How recruiting works
The Town Hall is where you recruit heroes. Each candidate shows a Spec., the category of the main Trait it brings (ATK, DEF, Explore, or Balanced). Explore Spec. heroes carry the town and Orchard traits (Gold, EXP, tree growth), so recruit them if you want gardeners. The cost scales with the grade of the traits rolled, and you can refresh to re-roll the offers.

How Tents work
Heroes deploy from Tents, and each Tent fields up to 3 heroes. As a hero fights it builds up Fatigue, and once tired it pulls back to the Tent to rest before rejoining the battle. Run more Tents to keep more heroes active at once. See Blocks & Chains for how combat works.

Fortress of Souls monsters
Soul-Stolen monsters are summoned from a separate Fortress of Souls tab at the top of the Portal's summon menu. To unlock it, build the Sanctuary of Souls and research Aura of the Lord of Souls at the Skill Lab; the tab then appears, and toggling it on swaps the roster below into the Portal. These corrupted mobs cost Gold to summon like any other, but instead of the usual loot they drop Crystal Powder, Crystal Shard and Crystal, the Soulbound material used for Transcendent weapons. They share the same crystals as the Soltar raid, so the Portal is a steady alternative to the raid.

How the Smithy works
Like the Bakery, it runs a Shop with the same system, but it sells weapons: up to 3 random weapons that restock automatically every 30 minutes, with a manual refresh for 100 Gold that doubles each time (resetting on the next automatic restock, and never resetting the 30-minute timer). See the Shops page for the full pool.
Its second button, Modify, opens the Forge, where you spend Gold to add bonus stat lines to a weapon. See the Forge page for the full pool of stat lines, slot counts, and forge costs.
Soulbound Weapon
Crafted and enhanced at the Smithy (its Soulbound Weapon button) from Iron and Soulbound crystals (from the Fortress of Souls raid or the Arena shop), plus a Gem cost (10 / 25 / 50 by grade) and Gold. Bound to its hero; not forged like an ordinary weapon or found as a drop.

Sanctuary of Souls
Max Lv.1 · 6-tile
Build a Sanctuary leading to the Fortress of Souls to challenge Soltar.

When the seal grew unstable and Soltar awoke once more, it was built on the outskirts of Hero Town under Archon's guidance, for a hero band that could not march to the fortress at the far edge of Valencia every time. Rebuilt with stones carried from the collapsed Valencia memorial hall, the building is new yet still bears its old scars.

ID: SOULFORTRESS
Lv	Upgrade cost	Build time	Unlock stage
1	1.000	-	-
How the Fortress of Souls works
Opens the Fortress of Souls raid: a three-phase battle against Soltar that rewards Soulbound materials (Crystal Powder, Crystal Shard, and Crystal).
Those crystals, together with Iron, are spent at the Smithy to craft and enhance Soulbound Weapons for Advanced Heroes.
